## 1. Scope of Human Expert Review

LinguaLens requires human domain specialists to evaluate and ratify all clinical stimuli used for Thai tone and acoustic calibration. Automated systems cannot determine cultural familiarity or developmental phonetic ease for young Thai toddlers.

---

## 2. Expert Review Criteria Checklist

### Criterion 1: Developmental Lexical Familiarity (Age 24–48m)
- [ ] Are target words within the spontaneous vocabulary of typically developing 2- to 3-year-old Thai children?
- [ ] Do target words avoid archaic, literary, or region-specific dialectal forms?

### Criterion 2: Picture-Name Agreement & Visual Salience
- [ ] Does each illustration unambiguously elicit the intended single-word target without prompting alternate synonyms (e.g. *สุนัข* vs *หมา*)?
- [ ] Are visual cards clear, high-contrast, and culturally familiar to Thai families?

### Criterion 3: Phonetic & Tonal Balance
- [ ] Are all 5 lexical tones represented with balanced counts ($\ge 2$ tokens per tone)?
- [ ] Are vowel lengths (สระสั้น vs สระยาว) and consonant classes (อักษรสูง/กลาง/ต่ำ) appropriately distributed?
- [ ] Are complex consonant clusters (e.g. /kr-/, /pl-/) minimized to prevent articulatory distortion in young toddlers?

### Criterion 4: Clinical Burden & Attention Span
- [ ] Can the block be completed in $\le 4\text{ minutes}$ without inducing child fatigue or non-compliance?
