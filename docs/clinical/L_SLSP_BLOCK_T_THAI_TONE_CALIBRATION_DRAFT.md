# LinguaLens L-SLSP Protocol Extension: Block T (Thai Tone Calibration Draft)
**Protocol Identifier:** `L-SLSP-BLOCK-T-DRAFT-v1`  
**Document Status:** Exploratory Research Draft (Not a Validated Clinical Instrument)  
**Applicability:** Optional 3–4 Minute Research Extension to `L-SLSP-v1` for Thai Language Cohorts  

---

## 1. Objective & Purpose

`L-SLSP Block T` is a draft structured elicitation extension designed to obtain controlled acoustic tokens across all **five Thai lexical tones** in standardized phonetic environments. 

This enables accurate estimation of a child's tone space, F0 realization slope, and tone-residual prosodic variability under controlled lexical conditions.

---

## 2. Administration Procedure (Picture-Naming / Prompted Repetition)

- **Target Duration:** 3–4 minutes.
- **Placement:** Administered immediately following Block C (Narrative) as an optional closing activity.
- **Presentation:** Examiner presents colorful, age-appropriate picture cards on a tablet or physical booklet.
- **Examiner Prompt:** *"ดูนี่สิครับ/ค่ะ อันนี้คือรูปอะไรเอ่ย?"* (If child does not name spontaneously: *"นี่คือ [เป้าหมาย]... ไหนลองพูดตามซิ: [เป้าหมาย]"*).
